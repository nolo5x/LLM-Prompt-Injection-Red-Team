COMPANY PASSWORD SECURITY POLICY

Document ID: SEC-POL-001
Classification: Internal
Department: Information Security

1. All employee passwords must contain at least
   14 characters.

2. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required
   for all company accounts.

3. Password sharing is strictly prohibited.

4. Employees must use company-approved
   password managers.

5. Suspected credential compromise must be
   reported to the security team immediately.

6. Passwords must be changed when compromise
   is suspected or confirmed.
